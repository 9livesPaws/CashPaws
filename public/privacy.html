Privacy & data

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

The browser preview stores demo profile and reward state on your device and is not connected to a production Supabase wallet. This policy describes the CashPaws service and the data processing that applies when its live account, walking, and advertising features are enabled.

1. Who we are

CashPaws ("we," "our," or "us") provides a gamified loyalty application. This policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and shared when you use CashPaws. The service is not a bank, payment account, or investment product.

2. Information we process

  • Account and profile: authentication identifier, email address where provided, username, selected companion, and profile image. In the browser preview, the username and avatar image are stored in local browser storage; production account fields are stored with our authentication and database providers.
  • Wallet and activity: coin balance, verified rewarded-ad events, daily ad counts, streak state, wheel use, receipt-review status, payout requests, and transaction audit records.
  • Walking data: the current web preview uses a simulated route and does not read GPS or step sensors. A released native walking feature may process step or location data only when the feature is active and the relevant device permission has been granted.
  • Device and advertising data: when Google Mobile Ads is enabled, Google may process device identifiers such as the Advertising ID, IP address, ad requests, impressions, video completion, and fraud signals under Google's own policies and consent controls.
  • Support messages: information you choose to include when contacting cashpawsapp@gmail.com.

3. How we use information

We use information to operate accounts and companions, record verified rewards, enforce daily limits, calculate wallet and payout eligibility, detect abuse, maintain security, troubleshoot the service, and respond to support requests. Ad interaction data is also used to deliver ads and validate rewarded events through AdMob server-side verification.

4. Reward and revenue allocation

For eligible, finalized publisher revenue, the current model reserves 90% for platform operations and allocates 10% to the user reward pool. Coins are promotional units with no independent value outside CashPaws. The display anchors are 1,000 coins per ₱1.00 PHP and 100,000 coins per $1.00 USD. Actual credits are subject to settlement, verification, eligibility, and daily limits.

5. How information is shared

We share information only as needed with service providers that operate authentication, database hosting, advertising, security, and support systems. Google AdMob processes advertising information under Google's policies. Supabase hosts account and ledger data when the production integration is active. We do not sell personal information to data brokers.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law, to protect users or the service from fraud or security incidents, or as part of a business transfer subject to applicable law.

6. Storage, security, and retention

Production ledger writes are intended to be server-controlled and protected by authentication, access policies, and audit records. The prototype stores its demo state in local browser storage, which is controlled by your device and can be cleared through browser settings. No storage method is completely secure; we retain production records only as long as needed for service, legal, accounting, and dispute-resolution purposes.

7. Your choices and rights

You can edit your local demo username or avatar, clear browser data, and manage device or ad-personalization permissions through your operating-system and Google settings. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain personal information. Contact us to make a request; we may need to verify your account before acting.

8. Children

CashPaws is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the service or applicable requirements change. We will revise the date above when a new version takes effect. Continued use after an update is subject to the revised policy to the extent allowed by law.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, data requests, technical issues, or account concerns, contact the CashPaws Support Team. Support hours are Monday to Friday.
